Subject: High Sierra format

There are individuals or organizations that have expressed a need for a
copy of the High Sierra format paper. In July 1993, I posted a notice in
selected newsgroups on the availability of the High Sierra format document.
I am again updating the July 1993 posting.

As there is no "official" source for a copy of the High Sierra paper, I
suggested in July 1993 that I could become the "official" source as I
wrote the paper. A few people have ordered the document from me. It is
not on the NY Times best seller list, however, it is needed in special cases.

	NOTE: For trivia buffs. Although the paper is dated 28 May 1986,
            it was actualy first distributed on 6 June 1986. In my haste
            to get it out the door, I forgot to change the date.

This historical ordering notice is no longer current.

For more information about this you might want to go to
